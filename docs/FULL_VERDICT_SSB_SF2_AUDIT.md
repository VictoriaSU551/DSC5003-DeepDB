## Point and interval evidence

The 431 required Q1–Q3 retained-sample groups and 109 Q4 two-SUM adapter groups all matched the independent exact sample SUM × N/n oracle within the declared original-double tolerance: 540/540 PASS. This is a sample-scaling correctness gate, not a claim that the small random sample exactly predicts its full population. The unmodified raw Q4 no-bound compound-SUM outputs failed that oracle in all 109 returned groups; those raw outputs and rewrites remain preserved. Native Q4 subsampling point predictions and the explicit two-SUM point adapter matched the corresponding sample oracle. The adapter has no combined CI because a covariance estimate is unavailable. Physical component SUMs have no matching full-population component truth and therefore have no invented population errors or CI coverage.

The 13 raw queries returned 540 groups per mode against 1,798 full local SF2 truth groups: 1,258 groups were missing, with zero extra groups. Missing groups receive an explicit 100% relative-error penalty. Both modes returned finite points wherever a group existed. In the CI mode the 13 raw queries had 345 finite intervals, of which 207 covered their population truth: conditional coverage **207/345=60%**. Another 195 returned raw groups had unavailable intervals, and 1,258 population groups were missing. Only 207/1,798=11.51% of all truth groups had an available covering interval; this last number is an availability-sensitive fraction, not conditional CI coverage.

Counting all physical calls, including both separate Q4 component SUMs, there were 758 returned rows per mode. The native CI mode had 513 finite and 245 NULL intervals. These physical-row counts must not be confused with the 540 distinct groups for the 13 raw queries or with adapter intervals. No missing/NULL interval is silently removed. A table choosing Q1–Q3 raw no-bound plus Q4 adapter as its corrected point series must label that choice and keep the raw compound outputs separately; raw no-bound Q4's apparently small full-population error does not repair its demonstrated scaling error.
